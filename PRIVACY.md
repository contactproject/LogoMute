# Privacy — LogoMute

LogoMute is designed to recognize television channel logos **on your own computer**, in desktop Chrome.

- The extension takes snapshots of the **currently visible TV tab** to look for the saved logo.
- Selected logo crops, positions, and per-channel settings are stored in Chrome's **local extension storage**.
- Temporary captured full-screen calibration screenshots are stored in **session storage** while you complete calibration.
- The extension uses no LogoMute account, external recognition API, remote image upload, advertising analytics, or tracking service.
- The **Watch channels** shortcut opens the external website **you choose**. By default this is `https://usnewson.com/watch/`. That website operates under its own privacy and usage policies; LogoMute is not affiliated with it.
- The extension only changes the **Chrome tab mute state**, not the system's speaker settings.

No promise of compatibility with DRM-protected videos is made. Screenshot capture may fail or show black frames for protected streams.
